LISTING TOOL - Quick Start
==========================

FIRST TIME ONLY
---------------
1. Make sure Google Chrome is installed (https://google.com/chrome)
2. Double-click setup.bat
3. Wait 2-3 minutes - it installs everything automatically
4. The app will open in your browser when ready

EVERY OTHER TIME
----------------
1. Double-click the "Listing Tool" shortcut on your desktop
2. The app opens in your browser
3. Close the black terminal window when you're done to stop the app

WHAT IT DOES
------------
Paste a realestate.com.au URL, get the listing photos and a caption
ready to post on Facebook and Instagram.
